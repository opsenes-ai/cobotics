# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the project and community
- Assume good intent

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report violations to **conduct@example.com** (replace with your real contact). All reports are handled confidentially.
